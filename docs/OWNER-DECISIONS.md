## Shipping

| # | Decision | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Processing time | **Ships within 2 business days** | The FTC rule requires a reasonable basis for any stated time; 2 days is realistic for a small shop. Without a stated time the default is 30 days. |
| S2 | Destinations | **All 50 states + APO/FPO/DPO; no territories at first** | USPS covers these; territories can be added later. |
| S3 | Free shipping | **None at launch** (revisit after you know your margins) | You asked for buyer-paid shipping. |
| S4 | Damaged-delivery report window | **7 days from delivery**, with photos | Short enough for carrier claims, fair to buyers. |
| S5 | "Delivered but missing" report window | **14 days from delivery date** | Matches typical carrier claim timelines. |
| S6 | Undeliverable / returned packages | **Reship for the cost of new postage, or refund items minus original shipping** | Common, fair, covers your postage loss. |

Already decided by you: U.S. only · buyer-paid shipping · tracking on every order · rates calculated by the selling platform · no guaranteed delivery dates.

## Returns & refunds

| # | Decision | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Change-of-mind return window | **30 days from delivery** | Matches TikTok Shop's standard U.S. window, so both channels feel the same. More than 7 days avoids California's posting requirement for restrictive policies. |
| R2 | Condition for change-of-mind returns | **Unopened, unused, original packaging only** | Squishies are squeezed and handled — hygiene and resale concerns. |
| R3 | Who pays return shipping (change of mind) | **Buyer** | Standard for small shops; TikTok Shop guides describe the same split. |
| R4 | Restocking fee | **None** | Customer-friendly; small amounts. |
| R5 | Refund of original shipping (change of mind) | **Not refunded** | Common practice; clearly disclosed. |
| R6 | Damaged / defective / wrong item | **Free replacement or full refund incl. shipping, buyer's choice; we pay any return** | Good will; carriers/platforms often reimburse. |
| R7 | Refund timing | **Within 5 business days after we receive the return or approve the claim** | The FTC requires 7 working days for canceled orders; 5 is safely inside that. |
| R8 | Exchanges | **No direct exchanges — return unopened + reorder** | Simpler inventory for a small shop. |
| R9 | Final-sale items | **Opened mystery/blind-bag items are final sale (only if you sell them)** | Prevents "open and return." |
